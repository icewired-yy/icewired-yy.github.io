Common Notes

  • Apple Music playback may require a valid Apple Music subscription.
  • Apple Music sync requires permission to access your Apple Music library.
  • Cadence matching works best when the iPhone is held in your hand while running or walking.
  • If a song cannot be played, it may be unavailable through Apple Music in your current account, region, or subscription state.
