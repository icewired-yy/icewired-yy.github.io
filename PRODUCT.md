# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is researchers, especially peers in computer graphics and
rendering who visit to understand Youyang Du's current work and interests.

## Product Purpose

The personal site brings together current academic results, technical blog
writing, and recent life photographs. It succeeds when a research peer can
quickly understand the author's research identity, find representative work,
and continue into the blog or publication details.

## Positioning

The site presents research as an ongoing personal practice rather than as a
detached publication index: rigorous graphics work, careful technical writing,
and ordinary life remain visibly connected.

## Operating Context

The public site is generated by the existing al-folio/Jekyll repository and
published through GitHub Pages. Visitors may arrive on desktop or mobile, often
following a paper, author, blog, or collaboration link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current factual content and existing content sources.
- Preserve the current Jekyll/Liquid data-loading behavior and public routes.
- Preserve the existing blog framework, including its specialized Markdown,
  LaTeX, bibliography, and technical-article rendering behavior.
- The visual identity, composition, typography, color, and presentation may be
  replaced completely.
- Keep the approved isolated prototype as the visual reference. Production
  routes opt into the same system through scoped layouts, includes, and styles
  while their existing Jekyll data sources and public URLs remain authoritative.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the current personal and academic facts, including the bilingual name.
- The visual character should be concise, contemplative, and personal, with the
  discipline of a practitioner rather than literal religious decoration.
- The graphics and rendering background should be present as authentic subject
  matter, not generic technology styling.
- The homepage must read immediately as a simple personal homepage, not as a
  newspaper, journal index, annual report, dashboard, or publication catalog.
- Keep the first viewport sparse: identity, research focus, one personal image,
  and minimal navigation. Publications, blog posts, and life photographs should
  unfold later with generous vertical space instead of competing in one grid.
- Express the contemplative character through proportion, light, silence, and
  subtle rendering behavior rather than print metaphors or literal Zen motifs.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current biography and portrait under `_pages/about.md` and `assets/img/`.
- Publications and previews under `_bibliography/` and
  `assets/img/publication_preview/`.
- Technical writing under `_posts/`, including the current LaTeX-enabled blog
  presentation.
- News, projects, CV data, social links, and the generated `gh-pages` history.
- Recent life photographs under `assets/img/`.

## Product Principles

1. Make research legible to peers before asking them to explore.
2. Let academic work, writing, and life feel like one continuous practice.
3. Preserve generated content and technical publishing behavior as the source
   of truth.
4. Spend visual boldness on one meaningful, graphics-specific signature.
5. Ship the approved prototype through scoped production surfaces without
   replacing the repository's content, rendering, or update pipelines.
